Manual & FAQs

How it works

1. Setup: Open the app from your Mac menu bar (top right corner).

2. Set the Time: Type your reminder text and pick the exact time you want it to trigger.

3. The Magic: At the set time, Doraemon will fly onto your screen from above to deliver the message with his signature charm.

Controls

ESC Press the Escape key anytime to instantly dismiss Doraemon.

Click You can also just click anywhere on the screen to close the reminder.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it completely free?

Yes! Doraemon Reminder is 100% free forever with no hidden costs or ads.

Does it work on Windows?

Currently, Doraemon Reminder is built exclusively for macOS.

Can I set recurring reminders?

Yes, you can configure it to repeat daily from the menu bar settings.

Why does macOS block the app?

Since the app isn't signed with an Apple Developer certificate, macOS Gatekeeper may block it. Go to System Settings > Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

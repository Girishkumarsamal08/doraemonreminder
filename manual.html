Manual & FAQs

Everything you need to know about setting up and enjoying Doraemon Reminder.

🚀 How it works

01. Setup

Launch the app. It lives quietly in your top Mac menu bar with a cat bell icon.

02. Schedule

Click the icon, enter your note, and choose the exact time or repeat interval.

03. The Magic

When the timer triggers, Doraemon drops down across your screen to notify you!

⌨️ Controls & Shortcuts

ESC Press the Escape key to instantly dismiss Doraemon and return to work.
Click Click anywhere on the screen overlay to close the reminder immediately.

💡 Frequently Asked Questions

Is it completely free?

Yes! Doraemon Reminder is 100% free forever with no in-app purchases, subscriptions, or advertisements.

Does it support Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3/M4)?

Yes, it is built as a Universal binary and runs natively at peak performance on both Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

Why does macOS Gatekeeper show a warning on first run?

Because it is an independent open-source release without an expensive Apple Developer ID. Simply open System Settings > Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway once.
